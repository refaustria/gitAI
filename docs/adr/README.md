# Architecture Decision Records

A decision in [`../decisions.md`](../decisions.md) is a *proposal*. Once it's
settled it becomes an ADR here: numbered, dated, and immutable.

**Why bother, on a solo project?** Because in four months you will look at a
choice, fail to remember why you made it, and either waste a day re-deriving the
reasoning or — worse — silently change it and invalidate earlier results. An ADR
costs ten minutes and records the *context and alternatives*, which is the part
that decays fastest from memory.

## Rules

- ADRs are **immutable**. Don't edit a decided ADR — supersede it with a new one
  and mark the old one `Superseded by ADR-NNNN`.
- One decision per ADR.
- Record the alternatives you rejected **and why**. That's the valuable content;
  the conclusion alone is nearly worthless.
- Number sequentially: `0001-short-title.md`.
